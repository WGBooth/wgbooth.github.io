I have begun my journey into networking and preparing for the CompTIA Network+ exam.

## Highlights

- Started learning networking foundational knowledge and key concepts using Professor Messer's CompTIA Network+ prep course lectures on YouTube
- Familiarized myself with Wireshark and planned to work through the Wireshark User's Guide this week

## The Long Story

My project has the dual purpose of preparing me for the CompTIA Network+ exam and building a portfolio project to show prospective employers. I'll be building a network traffic analyzer. Professor Guinn was awesome and helped outline a ten week plan. I began working networking foundational knowledge and key concepts using Professor Messer's CompTIA Network+ prep course lectures on YouTube. I've chipped away at learning the OSI model, some key port numbers, and IP addressing, just to name a few heavy-hitter topics. I already had Wireshark downloaded but updated to the most current version and poked around, just to familiarize myself with it. I plan on working through the Wireshark User's Guide this week.

I'm also trying to decide if I work in MacOS or if I use a virtual machine on my mac with Linux. If I were to go the Linux route, which distribution would be best aligned with my goals? From my research I was thinking RHEL or Kali maybe?

The biggest impediment is time management at this point. I am taking a very heavy course load to wrap up my last semester of CSPB, and I'm trying to get my feet under me after the first week. I think I could do a better job of outlining my weekly tasks rather than letting them bounce around in my head and completing them in an ad hoc manner. This week I've created a kanban for each of my classes in Obsidian to see what I need to be doing and strategize the approach.

## Addendum

I was wisely advised to use Ubuntu as my Linux distribution for this project. This is great, I'm already familiar with that distro and it should provide a solid environment for working with networking tools and Wireshark.
